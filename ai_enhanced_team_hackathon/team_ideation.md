Chosen Idea
AI-Powered Urban Issue Reporter
Problem Statement
Residents often do not know where or how to report local infrastructure problems. Reports may be incomplete, sent to the wrong department, or difficult to prioritize. This can delay the resolution of issues that affect public safety and everyday life.

The proposed platform allows residents to submit a description and optionally a photo of an urban issue. AI analyzes the submitted information, categorizes the problem, estimates its priority, and recommends the responsible service department. This helps make issue reporting more structured and supports faster, more organized handling of community problems.
MVP Features
1. Issue Submission — Users can submit an issue with a short description, category, location, and optional photo.
2. AI-Based Classification — AI analyzes the description and assigns a category, such as road damage, streetlight failure, waste, or water leakage.
3. Priority Estimation — AI suggests a priority level (low, medium, or high) based on the issue description. The result can be reviewed by an administrator.
4. Issue Dashboard — Users and administrators can view submitted reports and their statuses (Submitted, In Review, Resolved).
5. AI Report Summary — AI generates a short, structured summary of each report to help administrators understand the problem quickly.

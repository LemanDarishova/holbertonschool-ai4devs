# MVP Concept - AI-Powered Startup MVP Builder

## 1. Problem Statement

Many people have startup ideas but struggle to turn them into practical and structured MVP plans. They often do not know which features to prioritize, how to identify their target users, or how to define the initial scope of their product.

The AI-Powered Startup MVP Builder aims to solve this problem by helping users transform their ideas into clear and actionable MVP plans using artificial intelligence.

## 2. Target Users

- Aspiring startup founders
- Entrepreneurs with new business ideas
- Students working on startup projects
- Product managers
- Beginner developers interested in building digital products

## 3. Core Features

1. **Startup Idea Submission:** Users can enter a short description of their startup idea.

2. **AI-Powered Problem Analysis:** AI analyzes the idea and identifies the main problem it aims to solve.

3. **Target Audience Identification:** The system suggests potential target users based on the submitted idea.

4. **MVP Feature Generation:** AI generates a list of essential features and prioritizes them according to their importance.

5. **MVP Plan Generation:** The application creates a structured MVP plan containing the problem statement, target users, core features, and project constraints.

## 4. Constraints

- No user authentication in the initial version.
- AI-generated results depend on the quality of user input.
- The application focuses on generating basic MVP plans rather than complete business plans.
- Limited number of AI requests per user.
- No payment integration or subscription system.
- Generated plans can be viewed and copied but are not stored permanently.

## Conclusion

The AI-Powered Startup MVP Builder is designed to make the early stages of startup development easier and more accessible. By using AI to analyze ideas and generate structured MVP plans, the application helps users move from an initial idea to a practical product concept.
